Features

Everything a church office needs to run day to day — in one place.

💰

Budgeting & Chart of Accounts

Fund-based budgeting with a full chart of accounts, budget-vs-actual and income-statement reports.

🎁

Donations & Giving

Record donations by fund, print giving statements, and track pledges over time.

✅

Attendance & Check-In

Service attendance, small group meetings, and a dedicated nursery check-in/check-out flow.

👥

Membership Records

Full parishioner directory — members, visitors, officers, and children — with role-based visibility.

📰

Bulletins & Postcards

Print-ready weekly bulletins and outreach/thank-you postcards, generated straight from your data.

📊

Treasurer Reports

Fund overview, income statement, and budget-vs-actual reports formatted to match what treasurers already know.

🔒

Role-Based Access

Separate logins for Pastor, Elder, Treasurer, Data Entry, and more — everyone sees only what they need.

🌐
